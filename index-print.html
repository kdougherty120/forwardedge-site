/ 04 APPROACH

How we work.

Four moves, repeated on every engagement. No theatrics. No 80-page deck nobody reads.

i.

Listen at the source.

Two weeks inside the agency: shadow the operators, read the procurement file, sit in on briefings. No surveys.

ii.

Map the seam.

We render the system as it really runs — people, policy, platforms — and find the seams where change compounds.

iii.

Ship the smallest real thing.

A pilot the commissioner can defend in front of the legislature, the comptroller, and the constituent — in that order.

iv.

Run alongside.

We stay embedded through the first budget cycle, transferring the engine to your team. We leave when you don't need us.
